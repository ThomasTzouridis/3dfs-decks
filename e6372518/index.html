Idea 1: T-cell engagement visualized

One option: a short, scientifically accurate 3D animation of how Janux bispecific engagers work. A rough sketch of the structure:

1. Recognition

T-cell receptors recognize tumor-associated antigens like PSMA on cancer cell surfaces.

2. Engagement

Bispecific molecules bridge the T-cell and tumor, bringing the immune system directly to the target.

Idea 3: therapeutic index optimization

An example of a topic that is easier to show than to describe.

A core goal of immunotherapy is to maximize efficacy at the tumor while minimizing damage elsewhere. Standard approaches often hit a plateau where dose escalation increases both benefit and harm.

Janux's precision-engineered approach creates a wider therapeutic window, separating the efficacy curve from the toxicity curve. This is a visual story that is difficult to convey in text.

A short sequence like this could appear on investor decks, in presentations to clinical advisory boards, and in materials for potential partners evaluating the company's competitive advantage.
