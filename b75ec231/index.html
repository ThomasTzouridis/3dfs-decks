What we saw at Intra-Cellular Therapies

A neuroscience company founded on Nobel Prize-winning research, translating intracellular signaling science into novel treatments for conditions where psychosis, mood and cognitive symptoms overlap.

The platform centers on allosteric modulation of serotonin and other CNS receptors. CAPLYTA is their approved product for schizophrenia and bipolar depression, with ITI-1284 in late-stage trials for dementia-related psychosis.

Today these mechanisms are explained through the website with diagrams, scientific papers, clinical trial data and regulatory milestones presented as text and static images.

Idea 1: how the therapy works

One option: a short, scientifically accurate 3D animation of how CAPLYTA modulates intracellular signaling. A rough sketch of the structure:

1. Binding

Lumateperone crosses the blood-brain barrier and binds allosterically to 5-HT1A receptors on CNS neurons.

2. Amplification

The binding modulates intracellular cAMP signaling, amplifying and stabilizing neuronal communication pathways.

3. Symptom Relief

Dysregulated signaling is restored toward baseline, reducing psychosis, depression and mood instability.

An animation like this is usually built once and then adapted for different audiences. The next slide shows what that could look like.

Idea 2: one master, several audiences

Intra-Cellular Therapies' communications address five distinct groups. Examples of what could be derived from one master for each of them:

Patients and Families

Condition information and treatment options

  • A short explainer of how psychosis or mood dysregulation affects the brain and how CAPLYTA works
  • Patient testimonial or quality-of-life animation showing symptom improvement
  • Side-by-side comparison of untreated versus treated neural signaling
  • Dosing and administration guidance as an animated walkthrough

Clinicians and Providers

Pharmacology, efficacy data and clinical guidance

  • A mechanistic deep-dive on allosteric modulation and CNS selectivity
  • Clinical trial data visualization comparing efficacy and tolerability across indications
  • Mechanism-of-action video for use in physician education or grand rounds
  • Guidelines for patient selection and monitoring

Researchers and Academics

Patent landscape, novel mechanisms and future directions

  • 3D molecular structures and intracellular signaling pathway diagrams for publications
  • Poster and conference booth material on the platform and lead programs
  • Data visualization of preclinical pharmacology and in-vivo models
  • Timeline of scientific milestones and regulatory approvals

Patients, clinicians and researchers would all understand the same science in the same visual language, with the science adapted to each group's needs and expertise.

Idea 3: clinical pipeline at a glance

An example of a topic that is easier to show than to describe.

Intra-Cellular Therapies has built a multi-asset pipeline spanning approved products, late-stage programs and early research. Each program targets a different CNS indication, but they share the same mechanistic foundation.

Communicating how these programs connect, what stage each is in, and which indications they address is easiest with a visual timeline. Currently this is explained through text and tables.

A single visual could serve on the investor relations site, in research presentations, and in partner discussions without modification.
