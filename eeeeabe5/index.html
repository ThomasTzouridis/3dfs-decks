Idea 3: development and approval

An example of a topic that is easier to show than to describe.

Seres' VOWST program became the first approved oral live biotherapeutic, representing a milestone in translating microbiome science from concept to medicine. The company continues to advance multiple programs through Phase II and Phase III development.

Explaining the clinical development progression and the unique properties of live biotherapeutics is difficult in text alone. Visual storytelling can show efficacy data, timelines and the science that underpins each phase.

A sequence showing Phase I safety, Phase II efficacy, Phase III confirmation and approval could be used in investor presentations, regulatory communications and scientific meetings.
