01 · MODE OF ACTION ANIMATION

GH secretion, in motion.

In PGHD, inadequate secretion of GH from the pituitary gland leads to low GH and insufficient downstream signaling molecules.

LUM-201 acts via potent agonism of the growth hormone secretagogue receptor to increase endogenous pulsatile GH secretion.

One short animation for patients, families, strategic partners and advocacy audiences.
