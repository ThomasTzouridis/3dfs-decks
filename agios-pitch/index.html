Your science page carries the whole mechanism: pyruvate kinase, the final step of glycolysis, ATP falling, 2,3-DPG rising, a red blood cell that does not live long enough.


The pictures are two flat diagrams, a healthy cell beside one from a patient and a bar for lifespan. Nothing in them moves.


So the one thing that sets PK activation apart, that it gives the red blood cell its own energy back instead of working around it, is the one thing nobody gets to see.
