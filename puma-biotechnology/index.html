What we saw at Puma Biotechnology

"ALISCA®: ALISertib in CAncer." Alisertib is an investigational oral drug for HR+, HER2- metastatic breast cancer and small cell lung cancer.

Complex biology: Aurora kinase A, mitotic spindle apparatus assembly, chromosome segregation, cell proliferation and cell death.


On the website alisertib is explained in text, with a short Proposed Mechanism of Action list. No motion shows Aurora kinase A inhibition or mitosis.

A process like this is easier to follow when it is shown in motion.

How we could help Puma Biotechnology

01 · MODE OF ACTION ANIMATION

Aurora kinase A, in motion.

Aurora kinase A is involved in cell division and can contribute to the growth of cancer cells.

Alisertib inhibits Aurora kinase A, disrupting spindle assembly, chromosome segregation and cell proliferation, which can lead to cell death.

One short animation for oncologists, patients, care partners and clinical trial participants.
