THE SAME SCIENCE, IN MOTION

Your cell therapy technology page carries the whole route: collect, isolate and activate, engineer with the CAR gene, grow, infuse, and a median turnaround of 16 days in the US.


The five steps are flat icons, the films around them are gowned hands and a bag going into a fridge, and the two animations only ever show the finished cell.
