Idea 1: decoding thought to action

One option: a short, scientifically accurate 3D animation of how Layer 7 works. A rough sketch of the structure:

1. Recording

An electrode array sits on the motor cortex surface, picking up neural signals from thousands of neurons.

2. Decoding

A processor reads the signal pattern and translates it into intended movement in real time.

3. Control

Decoded commands reach the computer or robotic device, turning thought into action.

An animation like this is usually built once and then adapted for different audiences. The next slide shows what that could look like.

Idea 3: from one system, many capabilities

An example of a topic that is easier to show than to describe.

The Layer 7 system is not one application but a platform. The same decoded signal can drive chat, email, a cursor, games, or a robotic arm depending on what the user wants in that moment.

Showing this breadth of possibility is difficult in text, but a visual could make it instantly clear. One unified platform, many paths to independence.

This visual could be used on the website, in presentations to hospitals and funders, and in material for patients learning what becomes possible with the technology.
