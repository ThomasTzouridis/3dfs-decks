What we saw at Gossamer Bio

"A breath of new promise." A clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing seralutinib for pulmonary arterial hypertension and pulmonary hypertension associated with interstitial lung disease.

Complex biology: PDGFR, CSF1R and c-KIT signalling, vascular remodeling, inhaled delivery to the site of disease.


On the website this is explained in text and still images. There is no video or animation showing how an inhaled molecule reaches the lung and targets the mechanisms that underlie pulmonary hypertension.

A process like this is easier to follow when it is shown in motion.
