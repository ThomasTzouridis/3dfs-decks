What we saw at SENISCA

"Forward thinking to reverse cell ageing." Senolytic treatments that remove senescent cells, the cells that accumulate with age and drive age-related disease.

Complex biology: cell markers, senescence, inflammatory signalling, selective clearance.


On the website this is explained in text and still images. There is no video or animation showing how senescent cells build up or how they are removed.

A process like this is easier to follow when it is shown in motion.
