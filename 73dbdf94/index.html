What we saw at Nisa Breast Care

Nisa Breast Care & Cosmetic Centre, established in 2019 by Dr. Razia Bano in Islamabad, describes itself as the first dedicated breast centre of its kind in Pakistan's private sector and promises Comprehensive Breast Care and Beyond: screening with mammogram, ultrasound and MRI, oncoplastic and reconstructive surgery, 3D nipple tattooing and scar therapy, plus breast awareness lectures and self examination workshops. Your Breast Self Awareness page teaches monthly self examination with text and one awareness chart.

 

Why early detection lifts survival to 98 percent at stage 1 and 88 percent at stage 2, what a nipple sparing mastectomy or a lipomodelling reconstruction actually changes, and how to perform a self examination correctly are hard medical content to explain: to women who skip the exam out of fear or lack of confidence, to patients choosing between reconstruction options, and to the physicians who refer them. That is the part we do.
